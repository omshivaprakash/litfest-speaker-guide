## What is covered
Everything in the **`dataset/`** folder (the CSV files, `datapackage.json`, `manifest.json` and the changelog), and the same facts as they appear in the
app and the JSON files in this repository, to the extent we hold any rights in them:

- festival names, places, dates and official links;
- who spoke at which festival in which year (the appearance lists), and the stable person and festival IDs;
- our derived tables: dated prize wins read from bios, translators and language pairs, children's strands, press-coverage links, and the counts and statistics built from them.

## What is not covered
Other people's work stays with its owners and is **not** part of this licence, even where it appears in the app or in files in this repository:

- **photographs, logos, book covers and festival names and marks** (shown only to identify people and festivals);
- **biographies and other text written by festivals, authors or publishers**, and the text of news articles (the press table lists headlines and links as citations only);
- anything a festival or person asks us to remove (see below).

The code in this repository is separately licensed under the MIT licence (see `LICENSE`).

## How to attribute
Use any reasonable form that names the source, links to it, says the licence and gives the version, for example:

> Data: LitFest Guide (omshivaprakash), https://omshivaprakash.github.io/litfest-speaker-guide/, CC BY 4.0, version 2026.11.26.

Say if you changed the data. Do not suggest that LitFest Guide or any festival endorses you or your use.
A machine-readable citation is in `CITATION.cff`.

## Accuracy and corrections
The data is read from festivals' own public pages and from text in bios, so some fields are guesses (see `docs/FAQ.md` and `docs/DATA-DICTIONARY.md`). It is provided as it is, without warranty. The festival's own site is always the final word.
To correct something or ask for it to be removed, [open an issue](https://github.com/omshivaprakash/litfest-speaker-guide/issues/new) and say what and where.
