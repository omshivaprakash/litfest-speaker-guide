# User guide

This guide walks through everything in LitFest Guide, in the order you are likely to need it. No technical knowledge needed.

**Open it:** https://omshivaprakash.github.io/litfest-speaker-guide/

---

## 1. The home page

When you open the site you land on a page listing **every festival**, soonest first, each with its logo, dates and a countdown ("in 3 days", "Happening now", "Archive" and so on). Tap one to open it.

The row of round buttons near the top is the **festival bar**. Tap any festival name to jump there. Use the **‹ ›** arrows (or swipe the row) to see more. The **All** button takes you back to the home page. The **LitFest Guide** name at the very top does the same.

The four buttons at the top right:

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Search** | Look up a person across every festival and year |
| **Across** | See which festivals share the same speakers |
| **Explore** | Bookshelf, prizes, upcoming dates, bookmarks and more |
| **About** | What this is, how guesses are made, language, install, daily check |

---

## 2. Inside a festival

Open any festival and you will see its name, dates and an **Official site ↗** link on the second line. Tap the **☆** at the end of that line to **bookmark the festival**.

Below it are tabs. Most festivals have these:

### Discover
The festival at a glance: how many speakers, how many are **first-timers**, the share of **women** among speakers whose gender is stated, how many come from outside India, a **map** of where they are from, and a chart of **speakers per year**. Underneath is a row of photo cards: **Start here**, **New to this festival**, **Back again**, **Languages**. Tap a card to open that person.

### Who to hear
A short, ranked list of suggested names in each theme. Tap anyone to see their books and history.

### All speakers
Everyone, as a list.
- **Search box:** type a name, a book or a topic.
- **Filters:** country, language, first-timers, returning, with books and more.
- **☆** next to a name adds that person to your shortlist.
- Tap a row to open the **profile sheet**: photo, role, country, bio, books, and a history tab.

### Past years
This is the "what happened last year?" tab.
1. Tap a **year** (it opens on last year).
2. Read the summary line, for example: *"BLF 2025: 288 speakers on record (−63 vs 2024) · 94 had spoken before · 194 first-timers"*.
3. Use the filter buttons: **All**, **First-timers**, **Returning**, **Back in** *(next year)*, **At** *(current year)* **too**.
4. Tap a person to open their profile, with bio and photo wherever the festival published them.

If a festival never published a bio or photo for someone, the sheet says so. Nobody is hidden.

### The data
A short slideshow of charts and figures: new vs returning, gender, countries, books. Swipe or use the arrows.

### Tracker
Your shortlist: the people you starred. You can add people by name here too, or add ready-made groups.

**Programme-style festivals** (Samajamukhi, Jananudi, Book Brahma, JSW Sahitya Habba, Hay Winter Weekend) have a schedule, so their tabs are **Discover, Programme, People, Map** (where the data allows) and **Insights**. The **Programme** tab lets you pick a day and a venue and shows each session with who is on it. Tap a name to find their other sessions.

---

## 3. Search (top bar)

Type any part of a name. You get every festival and every year that person appears in, for example *Ramachandra Guha: BLF 2018, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2026 · JLF 2021*. Tap a festival to open it.

Spelling differences like "K S Nair" and "KS Nair", or "Abhijit V. Banerjee" and "Abhijit Banerjee", are treated as the same person. Other spelling variants may show as two people.

---

## 4. Across (top bar)

A grid with festivals along both sides. Each square is **how many people spoke at both festivals**. Darker means more. Tap a square to see the names. The second tab, **Most travelled**, lists people seen at three or more festivals.

---

## 5. Explore (top bar)

Eight views, each fitting on one screen:

| Tab | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Upcoming** | Every festival with a countdown. **Add to calendar** downloads a file with a reminder the day before. **Remind me** shows a banner on this site when a festival is within a week. |
| **Shelf** | A bookshelf of the speakers' books drawn as spines. Pick a festival, tap a spine for the cover, title and author. |
| **Debuts** | Authors grouped by the decade of their earliest listed book. |
| **Prizes** | Prizes named in speakers' bios (Booker, Sahitya Akademi, JCB and more), plus a timeline by year. |
| **Translators** | Which languages translators work from, and who they are. |
| **Trails** | Pick an interest (history, poetry, children's books, mythology…) and get people to follow, most-travelled first. |
| **Languages** | Languages speakers write or speak in, festival by festival. |
| **Bookmarks** | Your starred people (with books to read first) and bookmarked festivals. **Copy list** copies it as text. |

## 6. Moving around quickly

| Do this | To do this |
|---|---|
| **← →** keys (or swipe left/right on a phone) | Turn pages |
| **Shift + ← →** | Previous / next festival |
| **[** and **]** | Previous / next tab |
| **Esc** | Close a window |

---

## 7. Install it like an app, and use it offline

- **Android/Chrome:** open **About → Install app**, or use the browser menu → *Install app* / *Add to Home screen*.
- **iPhone/Safari:** tap Share → *Add to Home Screen*.

After your first visit the site is saved on your device. All festivals' lists open without internet. Photos and book covers appear offline once you have looked at them.

---

## 8. Language

**About → Language (beta)** switches menus and buttons to **ಕನ್ನಡ** or **हिन्दी**. Names, bios and data are never translated. These translations are a first version; corrections are welcome.

---

## 9. What is saved on your device

Stars, bookmarks, reminders and your language choice live in your browser only. If you clear your browser data or use a different device they will not be there. Nothing is sent anywhere.

---

## 10. If something looks wrong

Open any person's sheet and tap **Report an error**. It opens a prefilled message on GitHub. Or see [Contributing](CONTRIBUTING.md).
